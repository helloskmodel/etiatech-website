Excelitas has launched an enhanced generation of the **OmniCure AC Series** air-cooled UV LED curing platform, raising irradiance across the line-up and adding a 365 nm wavelength option.

For manufacturers running UV adhesives, coatings or inks on a production line, the headline changes are more cure power per head, tighter uniformity, and the ability to trim that uniformity **while the line is running**.

## The three systems

| System | Peak irradiance | 365 nm option |
|---|---|---|
| **AC8+** | 10 W/cm² | — |
| **AC9+** | 14 W/cm² | ✅ |
| **AC8+HD** | 15 W/cm² | ✅ |

All three keep the air-cooled, compact form factor of the AC Series — no chiller, no water loop — and remain modular: multiple LED heads can be combined to build a wider cure area while holding uniformity across the full width.

## What actually changed

**Module-level LED control.** This is the most significant difference. The system can optimise uniformity in real time, at the level of individual LED modules, **without taking the system offline**. In practice that means:

- Better than **±5% uniformity** across the cure area
- Far simpler balancing when several heads are installed side by side
- The ability to shape UV output for irregular substrate contours or a changing process width

Anyone who has tried to balance a multi-head array by hand, during a production stop, will understand why this matters.

**A 365 nm option on the AC9+ and AC8+HD.** Many UV adhesive and coating formulations are designed around 365 nm rather than 385 or 395 nm. Having 365 nm available on the high-irradiance systems removes a common reason to compromise on either the chemistry or the cure speed.

**Higher dose for faster lines.** The AC8+HD is aimed squarely at applications where line speed is limited by available UV energy.

## Energy and compliance

Excelitas cites up to **60% lower energy consumption** compared with conventional curing technology. For a line running multiple shifts, that is a real operating-cost line item, not a rounding error.

The regulatory side matters just as much. Moving from mercury lamps to UV LED means:

- No mercury-containing lamps to buy, handle, or dispose of
- No ozone generation, so no extraction requirement for it
- A cleaner answer when a customer audit or a corporate sustainability review asks about hazardous substances

For manufacturers already planning around tightening mercury regulations, this is the practical migration path.

## Reliability details worth noting

The enhanced series includes high-efficiency **VOC air-intake filters**. On a coatings or adhesive line, solvent vapour drawn through a cooling path is a genuine cause of premature optical degradation — filtering it is the difference between rated LED life and disappointing LED life.
