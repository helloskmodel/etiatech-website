**One-sentence answer:** Air-cooled UV LEDs need no chiller, run on 48 V DC, integrate fastest at the lowest cost and deliver 2–20 W/cm²; water-cooled UV LEDs remove heat through a coolant loop, deliver higher power (25–30 W/cm²), tolerate extreme temperature and dust, run quietly and reach 1.35 m in a single unit. Air-cooled is the default for normal factories; water-cooled for harsh environments or very high power.

## Why LEDs need cooling

A significant share of LED electrical power becomes heat; rising chip temperature lowers optical output and shortens life. Cooling efficiency directly limits sustainable output, which is why the Excelitas SLM technology lists cooling as one of its four elements.

## Comparison

| Item | Air-cooled | Water-cooled |
|---|---|---|
| Infrastructure | None | Chiller and plumbing (e.g. 6 LPM) |
| Integration speed | Fastest | Piping required |
| Peak irradiance | 2–20 W/cm² | 10–30 W/cm² |
| Environment | Normal factory | High temperature, dust, oil mist |
| Noise | Fan noise | Low |
| Width | Tiled modules | Up to 1,350 mm in one unit |
| Maintenance | Clean fans/filters | Coolant maintenance |

## Decision checklist

- No chiller, fast start-up → air-cooled
- Hot, dusty shop floor, quiet operation required → water-cooled
- Heat-sensitive substrate → both are infrared-free; decide on power
- Width over 1 m in one unit → water-cooled
- Fiber draw tower → air-cooled FJ228 or water-cooled FP300 depending on draw speed

## FAQ

**Q: Does an air-cooled unit blow heat onto the workpiece?**

A: The airflow is an internal heat-sink path and the source emits no infrared; workpiece temperature rise is far below a mercury lamp.

**Q: Can air-cooled LEDs run continuously?**

A: Yes; Phoseon air-cooled systems are rated 40,000+ hours provided the intakes are not blocked.
